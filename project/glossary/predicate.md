## Predicate

The predicate makes a statement (thus 'predicates') about the subjectthe part of a sentence or clause that contains the verb and tells us what the subject is doing or experiencing in the sense of an action, or being in the sense of a quality. In Gaua languages, definable as omnipredicative languages, members of all open word classes can act as a predicate.
