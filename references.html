References

⬇ BibTeX ⬇ EndNote

Cite a reference in the grammar by author and year, as you would write it: {{source: Smith 2020}} gives (Smith, 2020); with a page, {{source: Smith 2020, p. 12}}; as part of the sentence, {{source: Smith 2020, narrative}} gives Smith (2020); several at once, {{source: Smith 2020; Jones 2019}}. Two authors: Smith & Jones 2016; three or more: Smith et al. 2016. Add the letter when needed (Smith 2020b). The key shown below each reference (smith2020) works too: click it to copy its shortcode.

Import references

Choose one or more files in BibTeX (.bib), EndNote (.enw) or RIS (.ris) format, or paste the references. Nothing is added until you confirm: first you see what would happen to each one.
